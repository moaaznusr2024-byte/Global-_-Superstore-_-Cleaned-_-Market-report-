# Global-_-Superstore-_-Cleaned-_-Market-report- 
A Power BI analysis of a multinational e-commerce company dataset
Tracking over 51,290 orders across 140+ countries, the tabular dataset
simulates the Operational scope of a multinational e-commerce retailer. It
was originally sourced from Kaggle. The period from December 2, 2010,
to December 29, 2014, and contains 51,290 items in the orders
distributed across 25,035 unique orders across six market regions (Asia,
North America, LATAM, EU, WAEE, and Africa). It includes 3 categories. It
is divided into 17 sub-products. While the reported total profit amounts
to $1,467,457, shipping cost equals 92% of the reported profit (10.7% of sales),
and Shipping cost of 92% is estimated at $1,352,815.70, and this led to a
decline, or made the company's true net profit drop to $114,642 only, and that number represents 8%. There is another reason for this
percentage. There is another reason why this percentage reached this
level: Total Loss Amount orders are $920,646.16, and this represents a
percentage of 7.28% of total sales in all years. That represents 8.24% of
total cost, which amounts to $11,175,447.7.

## Key Result: 
1_shipping costs between total sales and total True Net Profit:
The reports indicate that 3 regions have high or soaring shipping costs,
and these regions are WAEE, Africa, and LATAM. Shipping consumes 92%
of profit, more than North America, the EU, and Asia. In terms of
shipping costs for each market, it amounts to WAEE= $88,375.73, Africa
= $88,139.47, LATAM= $234,133.62; the total is $410,648.82,compare to True Net Profit WAEE =- $44,477.76, Africa=+$732.16, LATAM=
+$12,490.13; the total is -$56,235.73.This is the strongest cause or
reason that makes the true Net Profit reduce to that level.
2_Losing Orders % :
Another reason is losing orders %= 30.12%, which amounts to 7541 from
total orders 25035. Consequently, total profit will decrease to an
estimated value of $920,646.16.
